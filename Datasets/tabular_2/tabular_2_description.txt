Tabular_2 Dataset Description
==============================

This dataset (tabular_2) was generated based on tabular_1 by introducing controlled distribution shifts and Gaussian noise to simulate inter-site variability in federated learning environments.

1. Source:
   - Original data: tabular_1 (diabetes_012_train/val/test.csv)
   - Label column: "Diabetes_012"

2. Modification process:
   - Six numeric features were randomly selected from the dataset.
   - Each selected feature underwent controlled distribution shift operations:
       • Random scaling in the range of 0.8–1.3
       • Mean shift drawn from a normal distribution N(0, 0.1σ)
       • Gaussian noise N(0, 0.05σ) applied at the sample level
   - The resulting dataset was re-split into train/validation/test sets
     with proportions 60%, 20%, and 20%, respectively.

3. Output files:
   - diabetes_012_train_shift.csv
   - diabetes_012_val_shift.csv
   - diabetes_012_test_shift.csv

4. Purpose and research value:
   This dataset represents a simulated “non-IID” (non-identically distributed)
   client scenario in federated learning. The applied distribution shifts
   mimic real-world data heterogeneity across different medical institutions
   or data collection environments.

   The shifted dataset is valuable for:
   - Testing model robustness under distribution drift
   - Evaluating fairness and generalization across sites
   - Studying the effects of feature-level heterogeneity in federated training

Overall, tabular_2 serves as an essential component for analyzing
how federated learning models adapt to site-specific data distributions.
